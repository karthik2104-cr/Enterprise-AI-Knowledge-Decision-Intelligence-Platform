# Benefits Guide (DEMO / SAMPLE DATA)

Northwind Analytics Group — 2024 Benefits
Synthetic demonstration content.

## Medical
Employees may choose PPO or HDHP medical coverage. The company contributes 80% of employee premiums and 60% of dependent premiums.

## Retirement
The 401(k) match is 100% of the first 4% of eligible compensation. Employees vest after 12 months.

## Wellness
An annual wellness stipend of $750 covers fitness, counseling copays not otherwise covered, and preventive screenings.

## Learning
A $1,200 annual learning budget is available for courses aligned with the employee's development plan. Certifications in security, data, and finance are prioritized.

## Family support
Backup childcare: 10 days per year through the approved vendor. Adoption assistance: up to $5,000.

## Enrollment
Open enrollment occurs each November. Qualifying life events allow mid-year changes within 30 days.
