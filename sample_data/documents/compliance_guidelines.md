## Major compliance requirements
Employees must follow these mandatory requirements:

1. Anti-bribery: never offer or accept anything of value to influence a business decision. Facilitation payments are prohibited.
2. Data protection: personal data must be processed under the company Record of Processing. Customer data stays in approved regions.
3. Insider information: unpublished financial metrics may not be shared externally.
4. Gifts: gifts to public officials require Legal approval. Other gifts above $50 require manager approval.
5. Conflicts of interest: disclose outside employment and vendor relationships in the compliance portal.
6. Records: retain contracts for 7 years. Do not destroy records under legal hold.
7. Speak-up: report suspected violations to compliance@northwind-demo.example or the anonymous hotline.

## Training
All employees complete annual compliance training by 31 March. New hires complete it within 30 days.

## Investigations
The Compliance team investigates reports independently. Retaliation against reporters is a fireable offense.

## Third parties
Vendors that process personal data must sign the Data Processing Addendum before receiving production access.
