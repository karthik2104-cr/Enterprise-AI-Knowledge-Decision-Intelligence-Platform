# Employee Handbook (DEMO / SAMPLE DATA)

Northwind Analytics Group — Internal Use Only
Version 2024.3 · People Operations

This handbook is synthetic demonstration content for the Enterprise AI Knowledge Platform. It does not describe a real company.

## Purpose
This handbook explains how employees work together at Northwind Analytics Group. It covers conduct, time away from work, reimbursement, and workplace expectations.

## Employment classification
Full-time employees work at least 40 hours per week. Part-time employees have a written schedule. Contractors are engaged through Procurement and are not eligible for full-time benefits.

## Workplace conduct
Employees must treat colleagues, customers, and vendors with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited. Report concerns to People Operations or through the confidential compliance channel described in the Compliance Guidelines.

## Leave overview
Leave entitlements are defined in the Leave Policy. In summary:
- Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave.
- Sick leave is 10 days per calendar year.
- Parental leave is 16 weeks for primary caregivers after 12 months of service.
- Requests should be submitted in Workday at least 10 business days in advance for planned leave.

## Employee reimbursement policy
Reasonable business expenses are reimbursable when they are pre-approved, documented with receipts, and submitted within 30 days.
Eligible categories:
- Client travel (economy airfare, standard rail, mid-tier hotels)
- Approved professional development
- Team offsite meals within the per-diem schedule
Non-reimbursable: personal entertainment, traffic fines, first-class upgrades without VP approval, and gifts above $50.

Submit expenses in the Finance portal. Managers approve within five business days. Treasury issues payment on the next weekly cycle.

## Equipment
Laptops and phones are company property. Lost devices must be reported to IT Security within four hours.
