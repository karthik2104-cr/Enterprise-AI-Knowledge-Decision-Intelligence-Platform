# Leave Policy (DEMO / SAMPLE DATA)

Northwind Analytics Group — People Operations
Effective 1 January 2024

This policy is synthetic demonstration content.

## Annual leave
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave each calendar year. Unused annual leave may carry over a maximum of 5 days into the next year. Accrual begins on the hire date.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave may be used for personal illness, mental health, or care of an immediate family member. Absences longer than three consecutive days require a clinician note.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers with at least 12 months of continuous service receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Leave may start up to 2 weeks before the expected arrival date.

## Bereavement
Up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave is available for the death of an immediate family member.

## Unpaid leave
Employees may request unpaid personal leave of up to 30 days with director approval. Benefits continue for the first 14 days.

## How to request leave
1. Open Workday and choose Time Off.
2. Select the leave type and dates.
3. Add coverage notes for your manager.
4. Submit at least 10 business days before planned leave.

Emergency leave may be reported by phone to the manager and recorded in Workday within 24 hours.

## Public holidays
The company observes the federal holiday calendar published each December. Employees required to work a holiday receive equivalent time off.

## Conflicts
Where this policy conflicts with local labor law, local law prevails.
