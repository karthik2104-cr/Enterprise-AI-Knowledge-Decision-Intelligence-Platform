## Purchase thresholds
- Under $2,000: manager approval and a corporate card.
- $2,000–$25,000: two vendor quotes and Finance review.
- Above $25,000: RFP, Legal contract review, and VP approval.

## Preferred vendors
Use the approved vendor list unless a documented exception is granted. Cloud infrastructure must use the contracted hyperscaler accounts.

## Conflicts
Employees cannot award work to a vendor that employs an immediate family member without Compliance clearance.

## Invoicing
Invoices must reference a purchase order. Goods are received in the procurement system before payment.

## Software subscriptions
SaaS purchases require Security review if the vendor will process Internal or Confidential data.
