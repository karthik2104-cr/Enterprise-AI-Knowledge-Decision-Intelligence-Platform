Classification: Internal demo document. Figures are synthetic.

## Executive summary
Q3 revenue was $13.888 million, an increase of 12.0% versus Q2 revenue of $12.4 million. Operating income rose to $4.678 million. Gross margin expanded to 61.4%. Headcount ended at 441.

- Closed two delayed enterprise renewals that had slipped from Q2.
- Expanded usage-based analytics in existing accounts, adding $0.9 million.
- Reduced infrastructure unit cost after completing the migration started in Q2.
- Customer NPS improved from 41 to 47.
- Support ticket volume fell from 1,840 in Q2 to 1,612 in Q3.
- Voluntary attrition declined from 11.5% to 9.8%.

## Revenue mix

## Expenses
Expenses were $9.21 million. Headcount growth of 13 employees was concentrated in customer success and security engineering.

## Major changes versus Q2
Revenue increased by about 12%. Operating income increased by $0.928 million. Margin expansion came from mix shift toward subscriptions and lower cloud unit cost.
